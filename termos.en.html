2. What the app is

The app shows weather forecasts, routes and warnings meant to support a motorcyclist's decision about whether, when and how to make a trip. It is a decision-support and information tool.

3. What the app is not

The app is not a safety instrument, does not replace your own judgement of real road conditions, and does not constitute professional advice of any kind. Weather forecasts are, by definition, estimates: they can be wrong, out of date or unavailable.

Routes come from open data and may not reflect roadworks, closures, one-way restrictions or recent changes. Always obey the actual road signs, which prevail over anything the app shows.

4. Safe use

  • Plan the trip before setting off.
  • If you use Trip mode, put the phone in a proper mount and use the voice warnings; don't look at the screen while riding.
  • For any interaction with the app, stop somewhere safe.
  • Respect the speed limits and traffic rules in force.

5. Speed-camera warnings

Legislation on warnings about speed-enforcement devices differs from country to country. In some countries it is forbidden and punished with heavy fines and equipment seizure.

The app tries to respect those differences automatically: it disables warnings in countries where they are forbidden and, where the law only restricts precision, shows wide danger zones instead of exact locations. Even so, it is your responsibility to know and comply with the law of the country you ride in. If you manually enable a feature marked as restricted, you do so at your own risk.

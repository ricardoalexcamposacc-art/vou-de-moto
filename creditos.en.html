LevanteMap credits and licences

The map you see in Levante is made of other people's work, almost all of it voluntary and almost all of it free. This page exists because the licence on that data requires us to say whose it is — and because it is only fair to say so.

The map

OpenStreetMap — the map data: the roads, the rivers, the towns, the street names, and also the fixed speed cameras the app shows. Cartography by OpenStreetMap, maintained by volunteers and available under the Open Database License (ODbL).

OpenMapTiles — the schema that turns OpenStreetMap data into drawable vector tiles. openmaptiles.org.

OpenFreeMap — the service that serves those tiles, with no account, no key and no request limit. openfreemap.org. It is the only provider the map depends on to exist.

MapLibre GL — the engine that draws the map on your screen, under a three-clause BSD licence. It ships with us, from our own origin: it is not requested from any third-party server when you open the app.

Natural Earth and Noto Sans — the base style of the map and the typefaces it uses; the typefaces also come from home, in a single family, so the saved map takes up less space on your phone.

Routes and search

OSRM — the fallback for route calculation: it is only contacted if the Geoapify key is missing. project-osrm.org, over OpenStreetMap data.

Geoapify — route calculation, place search and geocoding (turning a name into a coordinate, and the other way round). geoapify.com. Results carry the attribution of each source with them, and that attribution is saved alongside any place you save.

Photon and Nominatim — the search fallbacks, for when the main one does not answer. Both over OpenStreetMap data.

Overpass — the query that brings the fixed speed cameras from OpenStreetMap onto your route.

The weather

Open-Meteo — every forecast: rain, wind, gusts, temperature, thunderstorms, and the history that decides whether the first rain will lift oil off the road. open-meteo.com, under a CC BY 4.0 licence. The forecast is, and will remain, free in Levante.

Where you see this on the map

When you open the map, the credits appear in the bottom-left corner. They fade after about five seconds, or as soon as you touch the map — whichever comes first — because on a riding screen every line of text competes for space with the road. The ⓘ stays in the same corner, and that is what brought you here.

This is what the OpenStreetMap Foundation's attribution guidelines allow for interactive maps, and no more than that: credits may fade, but they must stay one interaction away. If you ever find the map without the ⓘ and without the credits, that is a defect of ours — and we want to know.

To tell us: info@getlevante.com.

The icons

Lucide — the icons on the bottom bar, the search magnifier, the GPS and the settings. There are ten of them, drawn as strokes on a 24×24 grid, and they live inside the app: they are not downloaded from anywhere, so that they work on the road with no network.
